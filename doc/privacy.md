# Rillvoro privacy

## Using the app

Rillvoro stores subscriptions, local playlists, settings, and enabled search and watch history on your device. When you export a backup, you choose where to save it. Anyone with that file may be able to read its contents.

Searching, streaming, downloading, and refreshing feeds send requests to the media service you select and the hosts it uses to deliver content. Those hosts receive your IP address and the information needed for the request, such as a search term or media identifier. If update checks are enabled for your build, the app contacts GitHub to check for Rillvoro releases.

## Sharing an error report

When Rillvoro encounters an error, you can review the report on your device. It can include the action you took, the request or URL, the service, your content language and country settings, your app language, the app package and version, operating system information, a timestamp, exception traces, and any comment you add.

You decide whether to share the report. You can copy its text and edit it before posting a public issue, share it with another app, or send it through your email app to a recipient you choose. A public issue is visible with your GitHub account name and any details or attachments you submit. GitHub handles information submitted through its site under its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
